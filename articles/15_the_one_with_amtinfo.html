About Amravati

Where Heritage Meets Modernity

🏛️

Historical Significance

Named after the historic Ambadevi temple, Amravati is the 2nd largest city in Vidarbha region. Badnera, once a separate town, is now part of this vibrant city.

🚂

Connectivity

Three railway stations: Badnera Junction (BD), Amravati (AMI), and New Amravati. Plus an operational airport with Mumbai flights!

🛍️

Shopping Hub

Known as a shopping destination with Tapadia City Center featuring all major brands. People from nearby towns come here for clothes!

🎓 Education Hub

From Schools to Premier Universities

🏫

Schools

CBSE: Podar International, DPS, School of Scholars
State Board: Abhyasa English School, Holy Cross, Gandhi Vidyalaya, and many more prestigious institutions.

🎓

Higher Education

Sant Gadge Baba Amravati University (SGBAU) - The academic backbone of the region.
Plus numerous Arts, Science, and Commerce colleges like Government Vidarbha Institute of Science & Humanities.

⚙️

Engineering Colleges

Government College of Engineering, Amravati (Autonomous) - Established 1964
Plus PRMIT, Sipna, HVPM and more technical institutes.

🏥

Medical Colleges

Government Medical College, PDMMC, and Dr. Rajendra Gode Medical College serving the healthcare needs of the region.

My Personal Take on Amravati

"I feel lucky to have been born here. Did my Kindergarten to Engineering all from this place. Amravati is neither too big nor too small - it's perfectly balanced. The city has great educational institutions, amazing food, and wonderful people. While it lacks employment opportunities currently, I'm hopeful that with the right leadership and development, Amravati will soon become a hub for industries and jobs. The future is bright for this city!"

- A Proud Amravatikar
